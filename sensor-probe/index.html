4 · Image container spike

Can a 144×144 image container have its pixels replaced by updateImageRawData alone — no rebuild, no flash? The page is rebuilt once on entry; every frame after that is a bare push. Judge by eye: does the swap flash, and is the circled arrow readable at this size? The dim frame answers whether grey level 5 is legible enough to use for staleness.

The pixel packing is not documented — if the arrow comes out as noise or stripes, switch packing and push again. Whichever one draws a recognisable arrow is the answer.
